## What Is a Spout Pouch?

A spout pouch is a flexible pouch with a plastic spout and cap. It is used when the product needs controlled pouring, resealing or easier use after opening.

Compared with rigid bottles, spout pouches can reduce packaging weight and storage space. They are also useful for brands that need lightweight retail packaging or refill packaging.

Common applications include:

- Juice packaging
- Sauce packaging
- Jelly packaging
- Liquid food packaging
- Beverage packaging
- Baby food packaging
- Detergent refill packaging
- Personal care refill packaging

## Common Material Structures

The material structure should be selected according to product type, filling weight, barrier requirement, sealing condition, shelf life and storage condition.

Common flexible packaging structures may include:

- PET/PE
- PET/VMPET/PE
- PET/AL/PE
- PA/PE
- PET/PA/PE

For normal liquid or sauce products, PET/PE or PA/PE structures may be considered depending on puncture resistance, flexibility and sealing requirements.

For products requiring stronger light, moisture or oxygen barrier, metallized or aluminum foil structures may be considered.

The final structure should be confirmed based on product testing, filling method and storage condition.

## Quality Control Points

For spout pouches, the following points are important:

- Spout welding strength
- Heat sealing strength
- Leakage resistance
- Pouch size tolerance
- Printing registration
- Cap fitting
- Puncture resistance
- Drop resistance
- Material barrier performance
- Compatibility with filling process

Liquid and sauce products may require extra attention because leakage, oil migration, poor spout sealing or weak pouch corners can affect transport and shelf display.

## Spout Pouch vs Rigid Bottle

Spout pouches are lightweight and space-saving. They may reduce storage volume and shipping weight compared with rigid bottles.

Rigid bottles may still be suitable for some products that require high shape stability, pressure resistance or specific filling lines.

The choice depends on product viscosity, filling process, shelf display, transport condition and brand positioning.
